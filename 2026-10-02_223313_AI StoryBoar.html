How to Use AI StoryBoarder

Getting Started

AI StoryBoarder helps you create professional storyboards from your visual content. Here's how to use it:

Step 1: Upload Your Content

Drag and drop your images or videos into the upload area, or click "Browse Files" to select them from your device. You can upload multiple files at once.

Step 2: Generate Storyboard

Once your files are uploaded, click "Generate Storyboard" to let our AI analyze your content and create a storyboard layout.

Step 3: Review and Export

Preview your storyboard and make any adjustments. When you're satisfied, export it as a PDF or save your project for later editing.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use high-quality images and videos for best results
  • Include visual variety in your uploads
  • Consider the narrative flow of your content
