How to Use StoryBoarder

  1. Upload your images and videos by dragging and dropping them into the upload area or clicking "Browse Files".
  2. Once your media is uploaded, it will appear in the storyboard preview area.
  3. Click on any item to add a title and description.
  4. Use the "Generate Storyboard" button to automatically arrange your shots.
  5. Click and drag items to reorder them as needed.
  6. Use "Batch Edit" to edit multiple items at once.
  7. When you're done, click "Export Storyboard" to save your work.
