Help Center

How do I start enhancing my VR experience?
Simply upload your VR game image or start with the default settings. Use the control sliders to adjust each parameter according to your preferences, then click "Apply All Settings" to enhance your experience.
What is visual optimization?
Visual optimization automatically enhances your VR game's image quality by adjusting contrast, sharpness, color balance, and other visual parameters to provide the best possible image quality on your device.
How does the sound environment simulation work?
Our sound simulation creates 3D audio environments that respond to your game's virtual space. It adjusts audio positioning, reverb, and other spatial audio parameters to create a more immersive soundscape.
Can I save my settings?
Yes, your settings are automatically saved to your browser's local storage. They will be restored the next time you use the enhancer.
Is this compatible with all VR headsets?
Our enhancer is designed to work with most popular VR headsets including Oculus, HTC Vive, PlayStation VR, and Windows Mixed Reality. Some features may vary depending on your specific device.
