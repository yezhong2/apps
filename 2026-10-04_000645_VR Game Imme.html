Help Center

How do I enhance a screenshot?
Upload an image, adjust the enhancement strength, then click "Enhance Now" — the canvas applies real brightness, contrast and saturation processing. Click "Download Enhanced Image" to save the result as a PNG.
What kind of image processing is used?
The page reads the actual pixels of your image and adjusts brightness, contrast and saturation on a canvas element. Everything runs locally in your browser — your image never leaves your device.
How does the ambient sound work?
The page synthesizes brown noise with the Web Audio API and shapes it through a low-pass filter — a real audio stream generated in your browser. Use the volume slider to set the level, and play or stop it at any time.
Does this need a VR headset or any other device?
No. This is a pure web page — it works in any browser on any phone or computer, and does not connect to any headset, controller or other hardware.
