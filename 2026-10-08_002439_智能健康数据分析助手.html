How to Get Started

Welcome to Health Analytics Dashboard

This tool helps you track and analyze your health data across multiple categories. Follow these simple steps to get started:

  • Navigate to the Health, Fitness, Nutrition, Sleep, or Mood sections
  • Fill in the required information for each category
  • Click the "Generate" button to get your analysis
  • View your results in the dashboard below
  • Use the "Batch Operations" feature to manage multiple entries at once

Understanding Your Results

Each analysis provides:

  • Overall health score (0-100)
  • Category-specific metrics
  • Visual charts showing your data
  • Personalized recommendations for improvement
  • Trend analysis over time
