Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the text summarizer? +
Simply paste your text into the text area and click "Generate Summary". The tool will analyze your text and extract the most important sentences to create a concise summary.
Can I export my results? +
Yes! After processing your data, use the "Export Result" button to save your results as CSV or PDF files. This is particularly useful for data visualizations and summaries.
How do reminders work? +
Set a task description and select a future date and time. The reminder will be stored locally in your browser and will notify you at the specified time.
Is my data secure? +
Yes! All processing happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to external servers. Your data remains private and secure.
How do I get started? +
Click on any feature card to start using that tool. For detailed instructions, check our user guide linked below.
