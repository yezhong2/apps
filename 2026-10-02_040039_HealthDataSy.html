Help & Support

How do I sync my health data? +
Go to the main dashboard and tap "Sync Health Data". Select the platforms you want to sync with and follow the authentication process.
Is my data secure? +
Yes, we use end-to-end encryption to protect your data. Your information is stored securely with zero-knowledge architecture.
How do I set health reminders? +
Navigate to the "Set Health Reminders" section, add a new reminder with your desired title and time, and choose your repeat preference.
Privacy Policy

HealthDataSyncer is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect health data from various sources including fitness trackers, smartwatches, and manual entries. This may include activity levels, sleep patterns, heart rate, and other health metrics.

How We Use Your Information

We use your health data to provide personalized insights, generate reports, and set health reminders. We never sell your data to third parties.

Data Security

All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption protocols. We implement strict access controls to ensure only authorized personnel can access your information.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time. You can also export your data in a portable format or request that we cease using your data for certain purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
