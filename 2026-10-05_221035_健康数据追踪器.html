What is Health Tracker?

Health Tracker is a comprehensive tool designed to help you monitor and improve your health metrics. It provides analysis and recommendations based on your personal data.

How to Use

  1. User Information: Enter your age and gender for personalized recommendations.
  2. Health Metrics:
    • BMI Calculator: Enter your height and weight to calculate your Body Mass Index.
    • Calories Burned: Select your exercise type and duration to estimate calories burned.
    • Nutrition Analysis: Enter your meal details to get a nutritional breakdown.
    • Sleep Quality: Record your sleep duration and quality to get personalized advice.
    • Vitals Tracking: Track your blood pressure and heart rate over time.
  3. Health Recommendations: Get personalized advice based on your entered data.
  4. Health Trends: Visualize your health metrics over time with the trend chart.
  5. Settings: Change measurement units or export your data.

Tips for Best Results

  • Enter data regularly to track changes over time.
  • Be consistent with measurements (e.g., always measure weight at the same time of day).
  • Use the export feature to backup your data or share it with your healthcare provider.
