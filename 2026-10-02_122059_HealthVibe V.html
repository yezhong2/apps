Squats Technique

Learn the proper form for performing squats to maximize effectiveness and prevent injury.

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Keep chest up and back straight
  3. Lower hips as if sitting in a chair
  4. Go down until thighs are parallel to the floor
  5. Push through heels to return to starting position
