    User Guide

    Assessment: Measure your voice health by speaking into your microphone. The assessment analyzes voice steadiness, strength, and duration.

    Analysis: Enter your voice data to receive personalized health insights and recommendations based on frequency, duration, and volume.

    Training: Follow guided breath exercises to improve breath control and voice stamina.

    Tracking: Record your daily voice usage to identify patterns and track improvements over time.

    Management: Based on your assessment results, create a personalized plan to improve your voice health.

    Quick Tutorial

    1. Assessment: Click "Start Assessment" and hold a steady "ah" sound for 6 seconds.

    2. Analysis: Enter your voice data (frequency, duration, volume) to get personalized recommendations.

    3. Training: Start a 60-second breath training session to improve voice stamina.

    4. Tracking: Record your daily voice usage hours to monitor your habits.

    5. Management: View personalized plans based on your assessment results.
