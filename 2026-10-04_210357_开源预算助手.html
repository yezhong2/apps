Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Your data is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device. We don't collect or store any personal information.

How often should I update my budget?

It's best to review and adjust your budget monthly, or whenever your income or financial goals change.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export your budget and expense data as a JSON file from the Settings section.

What if I overspend in a category?

The app will alert you when you approach your budget limit. You can adjust your spending or reallocate funds from other categories.
